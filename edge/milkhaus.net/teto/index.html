Why tipping is switched off

Our Ko-fi page was unpublished on 17 September and is under review. Until that finishes there is no way to tip, and we would rather say so than leave a button that goes nowhere.

The flag was for gambling. Teto has card and dice mini-games, and the old page leaned hard into a casino theme — the name, the artwork, the screenshots. That was our mistake in presentation, and we have rebranded it. The chips those games use are play money: handed out free by /daily, and they cannot be bought, sold, transferred or cashed out. There are no prizes, no payouts and no real-money wagering anywhere in the bot.

We have asked Ko-fi to take another look. Their stated turnaround is about seven days; we are not promising a date.

Also new

Server stats

Teto now keeps activity stats for the servers she is in: messages, voice time and members over time. In Discord, /server gives the overview, /me your own card, /top the rankings, and /stats and /chart the totals and graphs.

The same numbers are on the web at stats.milkhaus.net. Log in with Discord and pick a server. By default, only server admins can view a server's dashboard. An admin can use /stats dashboard to allow members or the public to view it.
