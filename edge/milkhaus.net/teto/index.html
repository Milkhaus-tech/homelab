01 / The resident

Someone to talk to.
A server she gets to know.

Mention @Teto or say her name. She answers in the channel, and sometimes chimes in on her own.

Conversation & memory

More than the last message.

Teto keeps an archive of the chat she can see and learns who people are over time. She can search the web when a question needs it, and see pictures, GIFs and stills from posted videos. If she cannot open something, she says so.

You can see what she holds about a member, remove a fact, or wipe your own archive and profile.

/teto-knows/teto-forget/teto-erase-me

A fact can be removed by the owner, its author or its subject.

Text-to-speech

322 voices.
Many languages.

Bring the text channel into voice. Teto reads a channel aloud, with voices you can browse before choosing.

/tts voices/tts browse

Always free in every server.

02 / Stats & levels

See your community take shape.

Follow activity over time, give regulars room to grow, and put the numbers where your server can see them.

Server stats

Activity, without reading content.

Hourly counts of messages, voice time and members, plus optional games and activities. Stats count activity; they do not read message content.

Get image cards in chat: a server overview, your own card and the rankings. Explore totals and graphs with custom windows, channel and member filters, a server timezone and CSV export.

/server/me/top/stats/chart/stats timezone

Or right-click a user or channel and choose “See user’s stats” or “See channel’s stats”.

Levels

Give participation a progression.

Turn on message XP and optional voice XP. Members get image rank cards, their own accent colour and a levels leaderboard.

Set role rewards that stack or replace, role and channel XP multipliers, ignored channels, and a custom level-up message and destination. Optional decay adjusts XP when members go quiet.

/levels setup/rank/levels card/rank-colour/levels leaderboard

Statdocks

Counter channels update on a schedule: member, role, status and voice counts; windowed totals; goals; countdowns; clocks; and YouTube, Instagram or TikTok follower counts.

/statdock

Statroles

Award roles for activity. Combine up to three conditions with AND/OR over a window: messages, voice minutes, invites, join age or holding a role, with at-least, at-most or top-N rules.

/statrole

In your browser

The bigger picture, on the web.

Log in with Discord and pick a server for charts, rankings, heatmaps and a levels leaderboard. The dashboard is admins-only by default; /stats dashboard can open it to members or the public.

03 / Server tools

Your server. Your setup.

Most server features are off until an admin sets them up. Admin setup commands require Manage Server permission.

Moderation

Set the boundaries.

An obfuscation-aware slur filter with escalating strikes, spam-burst timeouts and optional invite-link blocking.

Scan usernames, display names, nicknames and custom status on join and on change. Every moderation action goes to the audit log.

/mod setup/mod profiles/mod scan/audit

Optional AI hate-speech checks have a running cost for the bot owner, who controls whether they are enabled.

Rules & welcomes

Make the entrance yours.

Post your rules with a reaction role for members who accept them. Write and edit a welcome message in your own markdown.

The welcomer adds join, leave and DM messages, optional avatar cards with account age, and up to five auto-roles on join or after accepting the rules.

/rules/welcome/welcomer setup

Dangerous roles are refused. Accounts under seven days old are flagged on join cards. More than ten joins in a minute pauses greetings and alerts staff once.

Server log

Keep track of changes.

Log deleted and edited messages, joins and leaves, bans and unbans, nickname and role changes, voice moves, and channels or roles created and deleted.

Toggle events individually. Logs are batched so a raid cannot flood the channel.

/serverlog setup

Custom commands

A shortcut for the usual.

Create up to 100 commands per server, with placeholders for people, the server, channel, arguments and random choices. Run them as slash commands or with a chat prefix; the default is “!”.

/cc/cc use

Timers & polls

Keep things moving.

Schedule up to 20 recurring messages per server, from every ten minutes to every 30 days. Pause and resume them; a restart neither double-posts nor skips.

Run a poll, or pull up help in Discord.

/timer/poll/help

Play-money mini-games

A little downtime.

Blackjack, craps, slots, roulette, coinflip and crash. Ask to play in chat and Teto offers a game with a button.

Free chips cannot be bought, sold, transferred or cashed out. No prizes, no payouts, no real-money wagering.

/daily/leaderboard

Web manager

Make changes from your browser.

Log in with Discord to edit moderation, the rules post and its reaction role, or the welcome message, and read the audit log. Available to server owners and members with Administrator or Manage Server permission.

Posts and edits use the same code as the slash commands. Every change is logged.

Questions

The short answers.

Does this affect paid server tabs?

There are none to affect. While the free promo runs to 2026-12-31 no server needs a tab, and none has one.

Can I still use the mini-games?

Yes. They were never switched off, and they are free in every server. The chips are strictly play money.

Where is the source?

Teto’s source is public on GitHub. The repository name predates the rebrand.

Is my data affected?

No. The review does not affect the bot or what it stores. /teto-erase-me still wipes your own archive and profile.

AI chat keeps an archive of chat Teto can see. Server stats separately count activity without reading message content.

Can I bring my MEE6 levels over?

No. MEE6 does not expose its data, so Teto cannot import it. Teto uses MEE6’s level curve, with 15–25 XP per message at most once a minute and optional voice XP.
